# Legal framework

> Quelle: `legal-framework.pdf` (3 Folien). Manuell übertragen.

Non-exhaustive list created specifically for the challenge.

## 1. Data protection and appropriate information to the customer

Transparent processing of customer, financial and health data with advance information to the customer; personal data must not be transmitted to external AI models and/or cloud services in an uncontrolled manner.

## 2. Traceability of AI recommendations

AI recommendations must be explainable, documented and auditable in a way that is understandable for customers as well as internal and external auditors; each recommendation should be based on traceable criteria.

## 3. Human verification before a critical decision is made

The AI Advisor should not replace human review in complex cases or where there are significant implications for the customer (in both cases, based on predefined criteria).

## 4. Secure and regulatory-compliant implementation

Implementation of appropriate security measures (e.g. access protection, logging, encryption) and ensuring compliance with insurance law and contractual requirements (e.g. information obligations, formal requirements, anti-money laundering).
