# Auszahlungsplan: payout plan

Turns a lump sum into a regular income. Pillar 3b, single premium. Split between a guaranteed-interest part and funds.

- Investment: CHF 25,000 to 1,000,000 (incl. top-ups of at least CHF 10,000, max 5, during accumulation).
- Minimum forecast payout: CHF 2,400 a year.
- Entry age 18 to 75. Ends at the latest at 95.
- Guarantee share 0 to 95% of capital (max 50% from entry age 70).

**Fits:** retirement when the pension fund pays a lump sum, inheritance, sale of a business, bridging early retirement.

Source: [Tariff sheet Auszahlungsplan](https://assets.pax.ch/x/348092e417/private-vorsorge-technische-tarifbestimmungen-auszahlungsplan.pdf)
