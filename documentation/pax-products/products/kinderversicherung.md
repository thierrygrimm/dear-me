# Kinderversicherung (FirstStep)

Savings for a child, with disability cover for the child and protection if the paying parent (or grandparent) dies or can't work. Pillar 3b, periodic premium. Packages: Basic, Medium, Premium.

| | Child | Payer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry age | 0 to 20 | 18 to 65 |
| End age | 15 to 30 | max 75 |

- Minimum term: 10 years. Minimum premium CHF 1,000 a year.
- Savings part: FondsStar FR (funds) or LifeStar FR (funds + guarantee).
- Child: disability pension.
- Payer: premium waiver on incapacity and death, death capital (up to CHF 300,000 or 2× premium sum), disability pension.
- Package contents (Basic, Medium, Premium) could not be mapped reliably from the sheet.

**Fits:** new parents, godparents, grandparents (payer up to 65).

Source: [Tariff sheet FirstStep](https://assets.pax.ch/x/8db67e7c5f/private-vorsorge-technische-tarifbestimmungen-firststep.pdf)
