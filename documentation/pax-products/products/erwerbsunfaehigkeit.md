# Erwerbsunfähigkeitsversicherung: income protection

Pays a monthly income if illness or accident stops you from working. Pillar 3a or 3b, periodic premium.

| Tariff | Entry age 3b | Entry age 3a | End age |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SIRjL (young people) | 15 to 28 | 17 to 28 | up to 65 |
| SIR | 29 to 60 | 29 to 60 | 34 to 65 |

- Minimum term: 5 years.
- Pension up to CHF 36,000 a year as a fixed sum; above that, paid as actual loss.
- Waiting period for the pension: 12 or 24 months. For premium waiver: 3, 6, 12 or 24 months.
- Variants without accident cover (oU) or without psychological illness (oP).
- Crisis coaching up to CHF 2,500. Cover continues if you move within Western Europe.
- SIRjL: extra surplus in the first two years.

**Fits:** first job, self-employed (no or little 2nd pillar), sole or main earners, people reducing pensum (lower pension-fund disability cover), new parents.

Source: [Tariff sheet SIR / SIRjL](https://assets.pax.ch/x/12fee4331c/private-vorsorge-technische-tarifbestimmungen-sirjl-sir.pdf)
