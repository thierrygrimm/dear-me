# FondsStar: fund savings

Unit-linked life insurance; premiums go fully into funds.

| Tariff | Pillar | Premium | Entry age | End age |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FondsStar | 3b | Periodic | 0 to 70 | 10 to 80 |
| TerzaFondsStar | 3a | Periodic | 17 to 60 | 60 to 70 |
| Invest / PlanInvest | 3a, 3b | Single / both | see tariff sheet | |

- Minimum term: 10 years. Minimum premium CHF 1,200 a year (3a capped at the legal maximum).
- Death benefit: up to 2× the premium sum, max CHF 2,000,000; pays the guaranteed minimum or fund value + 5% of it, whichever is higher.
- **Birth or adoption waiver (PFZ, "Prämienbefreiung bei Familienzuwachs"):** Pax pays the risk and cost premium for four months after a birth or adoption. Available for 3a and 3b.
- Disability premium waiver: optional rider.
- Premium indexation (3b): +1 to 4% a year. 3a: automatic increase with the legal 3a maximum.
- Up to two funds; free switching; 1% issue commission on purchases.

**Fits:** long horizons (10+ years), younger savers, parents (birth waiver), people wanting sustainable funds.
