# LifeStar: savings with individual guarantee

Unit-linked life insurance that splits premiums between a guaranteed-interest part ("klassische Anlage") and funds. You choose how much is guaranteed.

| Tariff | Pillar | Premium | Entry age | End age |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LifeStar | 3b | Periodic | 0 to 70 | 10 to 80 |
| LifeStarInvest | 3b | Single | 0 to 65 | 10 to 75 |
| TerzaLifeStar | 3a | Periodic | 17 to 60 | 60 to 70 |
| TerzaLifeStarInvest | 3a | Single | 17 to 60 | 60 to 70 |
| PlanInvest (3a, 3b) | both | Periodic + single | as base | as base |

- Minimum term: 10 years.
- Minimum premium: CHF 1,200 a year (3a capped at the legal 3a maximum); single premium from CHF 10,000; PlanInvest single part from CHF 500.
- Guarantee share, chosen at signing: constant 10 to 100%, linear 0 to 40% rising, or dynamic 1 to 20% rising faster.
- Death benefit: 0 to 2× the premium sum, max CHF 2,000,000; pays at least the guaranteed minimum or the fund value if higher.
- Disability premium waiver: optional rider for periodic premiums.
- Premium indexation (3b): +1, 2, 3 or 4% a year. 3a: automatic increase when the legal 3a maximum rises.
- Up to two funds; switching free. Issue commission on fund purchases currently 1%.
- Interest assumptions in the calculator: guaranteed part 1.1% (low), 1.8% (medium), 2.4% (high).

**Fits:** people who want certainty, shorter horizons, people over 50, cautious savers.
