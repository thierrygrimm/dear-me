# Todesfallversicherung: term life

Pays your family if you die during the term. Pillar 3a or 3b, periodic premium.

| Variant | Pays | Lives | Min term | Sum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FK | Constant capital | 1 | 5 years | CHF 10,000 to 2,000,000 |
| FKF | Decreasing capital | 1 | 10 years | CHF 10,000 to 2,000,000 |
| FK2 | Constant capital | 2 | 5 years | CHF 10,000 to 2,000,000 |
| FKF2 | Decreasing capital | 2 | 10 years | CHF 10,000 to 2,000,000 |
| FR | Monthly pension to beneficiaries | 1 | 10 years | CHF 1,000 to 2,000,000 |

- Entry age 3b: 15 to 70. 3a: 17 to 60 (FK), 17 to 55 (FKF, FR).
- End age up to 85 (entry up to 60) or 80 (entry from 61).
- Premium indexation (+2, 3, 4%) only for FK and FK2.

**Fits:** parents (FR replaces income), home buyers (FKF follows the mortgage down), unmarried couples (FK2 covers both, no automatic widow's pension), self-employed with a business partner or debt.

Source: [Tariff sheet FK, FKF, FK2, FKF2, FR](https://assets.pax.ch/x/7eb5852589/private-vorsorge-technische-tarifbestimmungen-fk-fkf-fk2-fkf2-fr.pdf)
